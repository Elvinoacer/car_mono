# Premium Automotive Brokerage Platform - PRD Summary

## Overview
A high-end automotive brokerage platform (hybrid model: online discovery/reservation + offline transaction).

## Core Pages (Public)
- Homepage (ISR)
- Inventory Listing (SSR, dynamic filters)
- Vehicle Detail Page (VDP) (SSR + ISR, 360 tour, VR hotspots)
- Reservation Flow (Auth-gated, Stripe)
- Trade-In Valuation
- Finance Calculator

## Core Pages (Admin)
- Dashboard (KPIs, Charts)
- Inventory Management (VIN decoding, Media manager)
- Lead Management (SLA tracking)
- Reservations & Deals
- Financials & Accounting Sync (Xero)

## Visual Identity
- Premium, luxury, confident, buyer-focused.
- Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui.
